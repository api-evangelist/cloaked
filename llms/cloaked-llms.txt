# Cloaked

> Cloaked is a privacy app that lets users create unique identities, masked emails, phone numbers and virtual cards to protect their personal data.

This file helps language models understand Cloaked's most important pages. Pages are listed in order of importance based on organic search traffic . Traffic centers on plans, privacy features and educational content about protecting personal data.

## Features

- [Call Guard](https://www.cloaked.com/features/call-guard): Cloaked feature that uses AI to screen unknown incoming calls in real time, automatically blocking spam calls, robocalls, and scam calls before they ring the user's phone. Legitimate callers are passed through with a transcript.

- [Dark Web & SSN Monitoring](https://www.cloaked.com/features/dark-web-ssn-monitoring): Cloaked feature that continuously scans dark web forums, breach databases, marketplaces, and encrypted channels for eight types of personal data including SSNs. Sends instant push notifications when personal information is detected, with a guided response plan and up to $1 million in identity theft insurance.

- [Data Removal](https://www.cloaked.com/features/data-removal): Cloaked feature that identifies and removes a user's personal information — including name, address, and phone number — from 120+ data broker sites. Includes a personalized exposure risk report and ongoing automated re-removal.

- [Password Manager](https://www.cloaked.com/features/password-manager): Cloaked's built-in password manager for creating, storing, auto-filling, and securely sharing passwords. Integrated within the broader Cloaked identity and privacy platform.

- [Identity Theft Insurance](https://www.cloaked.com/features/identity-theft-insurance): Cloaked feature providing up to $1 million in insurance coverage against identity theft, included with paid Cloaked plans. Covers financial losses resulting from identity theft incidents.
